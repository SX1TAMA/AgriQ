# 🌾 AgriQ

### Smart Queues for Efficient Farmer Procurement

AgriQ is a smart automation platform designed to improve the agricultural procurement process for farmers. It addresses common problems such as long waiting times, overcrowded procurement centers, unclear schedules, and lack of real-time procurement updates.

AgriQ uses **digital registration, automated scheduling, smart queue management, real-time monitoring, notifications, and intelligent optimization** to make farmer procurement faster, more organized, and transparent.

---

## 🚜 Problem

Farmers often face several challenges during the procurement process:

- ⏳ Long waiting times at procurement centers
- 👥 Overcrowded procurement centers
- 📅 Unclear procurement schedules
- 🎫 Manual queue and token management
- 📱 Lack of real-time procurement information
- 🚚 Unnecessary trips to procurement centers
- ⚠️ Poor coordination between farmers and authorities

These problems lead to wasted time, additional costs, and an inefficient procurement process.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**AgriQ** replaces the traditional physical queue with a **smart digital queue and automated scheduling system**.

The platform allows farmers to:

- Register their produce digitally
- Receive an automated procurement slot
- Get a digital queue token
- Track their queue position
- View estimated waiting time
- Receive real-time notifications
- Track procurement status

At the same time, authorities can monitor procurement centers, manage queues, track capacity, and optimize the procurement process.
